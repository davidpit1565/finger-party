How to use Finger Picker, and what to do if something doesn't work.

How to use it

  1. Open the app. It starts straight on the play screen.
  2. Everyone puts one finger on the screen. At least two fingers are needed.
  3. Keep your fingers still. A short countdown starts once the group has settled. If someone new joins during the countdown, it restarts so they are included.
  4. When the round locks, no new fingers can join. One finger is picked at random and its color fills the screen.
  5. Lift all fingers, then tap Play again.

Tap the gear icon on the play screen to open Settings, where you can turn Sound and Haptics on or off.

FAQ

How many fingers can it track at once?

This depends on your iPhone model and iOS version, and it is a hardware and system limit, not an app setting. We do not publish a guaranteed number. If you place more fingers than your device can track, the app shows rings only for the touches iOS reports and picks only among those.

Is the pick really random?

Yes. Once the group is locked, the app picks one finger from that group using the system random number generator. The choice is made before the animation starts and does not depend on touch order, screen position, or color. The animation only shows the result.

Fewer rings appear than there are fingers.

You may have reached your device's multi-touch limit. Try spreading fingers apart, and make sure no palm or thumb is resting on the screen. A thick screen protector or wet fingers can also affect touch detection.

The round restarts or a ring disappears.

If a finger is lifted before the round locks, it is removed from the group. If iOS interrupts the app (a notification, Control Center, a call, or switching apps), the round is cancelled and the app returns to the start screen. Try keeping fingers away from the screen edges, where iOS system gestures can take over.

There is no sound.

Sound is off by default. Turn it on in Settings. Also check that your iPhone is not in silent mode and the volume is up.

There is no vibration.

Check that Haptics is on in the app's Settings. Haptics also depend on your iPhone's hardware and system settings, including System Haptics in the iOS Sounds and Haptics settings.

Can I reduce the animation?

Yes. If Reduce Motion is turned on in iOS Accessibility settings, the app uses a shorter, simpler reveal. The result is the same.

Does it need an internet connection or an account?

No. See the Privacy Policy.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone only, with iOS 17 or later. The app is in English.
