Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-04

Short version: Finger Picker does not collect, store, or send any personal data. It does not connect to the internet.

1. What the app does with your data

Finger Picker is a fully offline app. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs. It makes no network calls. The developer receives no data from the app.

2. Touch positions

To work, the app reads where your fingers touch the screen during a round. These positions are held only temporarily in the device's memory while the round is on screen. They are never saved to storage and never sent anywhere. When the round ends or the app is closed, they are gone.

3. Settings stored on your device

The app stores exactly three preferences on your device, using the standard iOS local storage for app settings (UserDefaults): whether Sound is on, whether Haptics is on, and which finger color mood (color palette) you chose. These contain no personal information, stay on your device, and are removed if you delete the app.

4. Sound and haptics

The app plays short sounds that are bundled inside the app and uses your iPhone's haptic feedback. It does not use the microphone, camera, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal data on your device, and it does not request permission for any of them.

5. No third parties

The app contains no third-party code that collects data, so no data is shared with, sold to, or disclosed to any third party by the app.

6. Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children. It is not designed specifically for children and is not in the App Store Kids category.

7. Apple and GitHub

  • If you download the app from the App Store, Apple handles the download and any purchase or account information under Apple's own privacy policy. The developer does not receive that information through the app. If you have chosen in iOS to share analytics or crash data with app developers, Apple may make aggregated reports available to the developer; this is controlled by your iOS settings, not by the app.
  • This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visits to the site under GitHub's own privacy statement. The site itself has no scripts, cookies, trackers, or external fonts.
  • If you open a support issue on GitHub, what you post there is public and handled under GitHub's terms and privacy statement. Please do not post personal information.

8. Changes to this policy

If the app's behavior changes in a way that affects privacy, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy: please open an issue on GitHub. A support email address will be added to this page when it is available.
