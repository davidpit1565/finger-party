Everyone touches. One is picked.

Finger Picker is a simple iPhone app for deciding who goes first, who pays, or who does the dishes. Everyone puts a finger on the screen and the app picks one at random.

What it does

  1. Place two or more fingers on the screen. Each finger gets its own glowing ring.
  2. Hold still for a moment. A short countdown starts, and anyone who joins in time is included.
  3. When the countdown ends, the group is locked in and one finger is chosen at random from everyone who was touching.
  4. The winning finger's color spreads out from where it touched and fills the screen.
  5. Lift your fingers and play again.

Sound and haptic feedback can each be switched on or off in Settings.

Privacy first. The app works fully offline, has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party SDKs, and never sends anything off your device. Read the Privacy Policy.

Details

  • iPhone only, iOS 17 or later.
  • English.
  • Portrait and landscape.
  • The number of fingers that can be tracked at once depends on your iPhone and iOS. See Support.
